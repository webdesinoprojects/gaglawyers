# Services Page Admin - Quick Start Checklist ✅

## 🚀 Getting Started (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Access Admin Panel
- [ ] Go to `/admin/services`
- [ ] Login if needed
- [ ] See list of services in left sidebar

### Step 2: Select a Service
- [ ] Click any service from the list
- [ ] Wait for service data to load
- [ ] See three tabs: Sections, **Services Page**, SEO & Settings

### Step 3: Open Services Page Tab
- [ ] Click the **"Services Page"** tab
- [ ] See the new management interface
- [ ] Scroll through all sections

---

## 📝 Essential Setup (Per Service)

### Visibility (Required)
- [ ] ✅ Enable "Show on Services Page"
- [ ] Set Display Priority number (lower = first)
- [ ] Decide if service should be Featured

### Content (Required)
- [ ] Write Card Summary (150-200 characters)
- [ ] Review Card Title (or leave empty for auto)

### Image (Required)
- [ ] Click a suggested Unsplash search term
- [ ] Find and copy image URL
- [ ] Paste in Image URL field
- [ ] Click Preview to verify
- [ ] Add Image Alt Text

### Badges (Optional)
- [ ] Keep "Expert Team" badge enabled (default)
- [ ] Keep "Proven Results" badge enabled (default)

### Save
- [ ] Click "Save changes" button
- [ ] Wait for success message
- [ ] Visit `/services` to verify

---

## 🎯 Quick Image Guide

### Finding Images on Unsplash:

1. **Click suggested term** in admin panel
2. **Browse images** on Unsplash
3. **Right-click image** you like
4. **Select "Copy image address"**
5. **Paste in admin panel**
6. **Click Preview** to check
7. **Add alt text** (describe the image)
8. **Save**

### Image Tips:
- ✅ Use landscape orientation
- ✅ Choose professional, relevant images
- ✅ Ensure good lighting and clarity
- ✅ Avoid text-heavy images
- ❌ Don't use portrait orientation
- ❌ Avoid blurry or low-quality images

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Leaving services without images**
- Every active service needs an image
- Use Unsplash suggestions

❌ **Writing too much in Card Summary**
- Keep it under 200 characters
- Focus on key benefits

❌ **Not setting Display Order**
- Services appear randomly
- Set strategic order (1, 2, 3...)

❌ **Featuring too many services**
- Spotlight loses impact
- Stick to 2-4 featured services

❌ **Forgetting to Save**
- Always click Save button
- Wait for success message

❌ **Not testing on mobile**
- Check responsive design
- Verify images scale properly

## 🎯 Content Writing Tips

### Card Summary Formula:
1. **What** (service type)
2. **Benefit** (what client gets)
3. **Call to action** (implied)

**Example:**
"Expert criminal defense representation with proven track record. Strategic planning and dedicated advocacy for your case."

### Good Summaries:
✅ "Comprehensive corporate legal services for businesses of all sizes. Contract drafting, compliance, and strategic counsel."

✅ "Protect your family's interests with compassionate legal guidance. Divorce, custody, and mediation services."

✅ "Navigate property transactions with confidence. Expert advice on purchases, sales, and disputes."

### Bad Summaries:
❌ "We are the best lawyers." (too vague)
❌ "Call us now for legal help with anything you need." (not specific)
❌ "This is a very long description that goes on and on about everything we do without focusing on key benefits..." (too long)

## 🆘 Quick Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Service not showing
**Solution:** Check "Show on Services Page" is enabled

**Problem:** Image not loading
**Solution:** Verify URL is HTTPS and accessible

**Problem:** Changes not saving
**Solution:** Check you're logged in, try again

**Problem:** Featured section empty
**Solution:** Enable "Featured Service" on at least one service

**Problem:** Search not working
**Solution:** Ensure services are active and have content
